# Delete your Gem: Incremental Idle account

**Geo Studio** · [Polski](./pl/) · [Privacy policy](../privacy-policy/)

## Request deletion without the app

Email **[contact.geostudio@protonmail.com](mailto:contact.geostudio@protonmail.com?subject=Gem%3A%20Incremental%20Idle%20-%20account%20deletion%20request)** and ask us to delete your **Gem: Incremental Idle game account and its associated data**. You can copy the address into any email service. Send the email to submit your request. You do not need the app or access to your original Google account to contact us.

If you have lost access to the linked Google account, tell us. A player ID can help us find the account if you know it. We accept an initial request without it. We reply privately to verify your authority and confirm which account you want deleted. A sender address or player ID alone cannot verify ownership. **Do not send passwords, tokens, sign-in codes, identity documents or private save files.**

## Request or cancel in the app

While online and signed in to the linked game account, open **Settings → Google Account → Delete Account**, then select **Request Deletion**. To withdraw the pending request, choose **Cancel Account Deletion** in the same place before deletion starts. After verification an administrator carries out the deletion and checks the result. If you sent an email and wish to withdraw it, contact us promptly.

## What is deleted

- The Unity Authentication game account and its Google sign-in link. You keep your Google account.
- Your private cloud save content and the associated account-specific transaction entries.
- Your live ranking profile, public ranking ID, current nickname reservation, scores and history. Older table copies may remain on viewers’ devices.
- Your entry in the pending requests register once deletion is complete.

## What remains, and why

- Minimal private markers identify the deleted save or ranking profile and prevent stale writes or restoration. They contain no progress or scores. Before removal can be approved, the old account must be gone, old sessions and pending writes must have ended and controlled copies must have expired or be protected against restoring the data. There is no automatic expiry.
- A name blocked by moderation can remain on a shared list to prevent reuse. That list contains the blocked text without the account profile.
- We keep correspondence for the duration of the request and **at most 12 months after closure**, unless a legal obligation or a specific legal claim requires longer. We remove unnecessary material sooner. The administrator keeps a private plan, work log and verification evidence to complete or safely resume deletion and check its outcome. At closure we assess what evidence is still necessary for the applicable obligation or claim and minimise it accordingly. We explain relevant exceptions when reporting the result.
- Device saves, Android backups and Unity’s internal technical copies are outside the live deletion procedure. Clearing app storage can permanently remove your local progress. Android backups have separate controls. We cannot confirm the expiry date of a particular Unity technical copy. Advertising providers’ records may use other identifiers. Contact us for help with those records or with a guest-only ranking identity.
