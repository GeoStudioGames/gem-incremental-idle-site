## 2. Gameplay Data and Android Backup

You do not need a player account. The game stores progress, settings, gameplay statistics and timestamps on your device. Local save backups may also be stored.

These data help preserve your progress, remember selected preferences, calculate offline progress and recover saves. During ordinary gameplay, Geo Studio does not normally receive or associate local gameplay data with an identified player. Local storage alone does not make information anonymous. If you voluntarily send a save or log linked to you with a support request, the support rules below apply.

Android system backup is enabled for the current test. Depending on platform, device, account and user settings, Android may copy, restore or transfer eligible app data, including progress, settings and save copies. Google or another platform provider may handle those backups. This does not mean the game developer receives a copy. Restoration of every save is not guaranteed. See [Google's Android backup information](https://support.google.com/android/answer/2819582?hl=en).

The game has no game-operated cloud-save service. Google Play Games Cloud Save is only a future plan and is not implemented.

Optional Unity Engine Diagnostics, Analytics, Crash Reporting and Performance Reporting are disabled for the intended first Android test build. Local logs and manual bug reports remain available. These settings do not control advertising or Android services and do not mean that the app makes no network connections.

## 3. Support and Voluntary Bug Reports

When you contact us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send, such as screenshots, logs or saves. The game does not automatically upload bug reports or attach files.

We use relevant information to answer requests, investigate reported problems and maintain necessary follow-up and continuity. The basis is our legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Please send only what is needed. Do not send passwords or unnecessary information about other people. Remove personal details from screenshots and agree with us on what is needed before sending logs or saves. We request and retain attachments only where relevant and necessary. Unnecessary attachments are deleted earlier.

## 4. Advertising and Privacy Choices

Rewarded advertising is planned through Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads and related advertising services. Google UMP presents and manages privacy choices; Google Mobile Ads is not the game's ad-serving system. **Ads currently remain blocked.** The European privacy message has not yet been published.

Once available, the form will allow you to consent, refuse or select Manage options. You will be able to revisit choices through Privacy preferences in Settings when available. **Without the required advertising consent, no advertising requests are made.** There is no non-personalized-ad fallback after refusal.

Normal gameplay and ordinary offline rewards remain available without advertising consent. Opening the game, pressing a rewarded-ad button, participating in testing or reading this policy does not constitute that consent.

Personalized and other nonessential advertising processing relies on consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR where applicable. Nonessential storage or access on your device also requires applicable prior consent under Polish electronic-communications rules. Necessary handling of information to explain, record and respect privacy choices is separate and is based on our legal obligations under Article 6(1)(c) GDPR.

Advertising and privacy providers may process IP addresses, device/app information, permitted device or advertising identifiers, ad delivery and interaction events, and privacy-choice information. Which data are involved depends on the service, configuration and choices; not every category is always transmitted. UMP communication may occur before advertising consent. This does not authorize unrelated pre-choice processing. Providers may process technical and ad-related data under their applicable privacy terms and legal bases.

## 7. How Long Data Is Kept

Local gameplay data and save copies remain until overwritten or deleted. Clearing app data or uninstalling normally removes local files and may lose your progress. Platform backups may independently preserve or restore eligible data; uninstalling does not guarantee immediate deletion of those copies.

Support and privacy correspondence is retained for **no longer than 12 months after the matter is closed**, unless longer retention is necessary to comply with a legal obligation or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. This is a maximum, not a requirement to keep every item for that period. Attachments are deleted earlier when no longer necessary, subject to those exceptions.

Advertising/privacy providers retain data according to applicable service, purpose, legal, security and retention rules. There is no universal period for all provider data or consent records. See [Google's retention information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention) and the provider notices above. Our correspondence limit does not set their retention periods.

## 8. Your Rights and Requests

Under the GDPR, you may request access, rectification, erasure or restriction. Portability applies where its conditions are met. You may object to legitimate-interest processing on grounds relating to your situation. You may withdraw consent without affecting the lawfulness of earlier consent-based processing.

Send requests to **contact.geostudio@protonmail.com**. Jacek Kocemba handles them personally, acknowledges receipt and requests clarification if necessary. Identity checks will be proportionate to the request and risk. Additional identifying information is requested only when needed to resolve reasonable doubts about identity. Identity documents are not requested routinely or without a justified, proportionate need.

Requests are handled without undue delay, normally within one month. Where complexity or the number of requests permits a GDPR extension of up to two further months, we will explain it within the first month. If we cannot act, we will explain why and describe complaint and court remedies within that month. Requests are normally free, subject to statutory exceptions for manifestly unfounded or excessive requests.

We handle data under our control and, where needed, cooperate with providers or explain how to contact an independent controller. Applicable legal exceptions to deletion remain valid. Necessary handling of statutory GDPR requests relies on Article 6(1)(c), together with Articles 12–22 GDPR.

You may complain to the **President of the Polish Personal Data Protection Office (UODO)**. See [UODO's rights information](https://uodo.gov.pl/pl/493/155) and [complaint guidance](https://uodo.gov.pl/pl/526/2464).
